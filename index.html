Undergraduate Thesis & Industrial Case Study

SISTEM PENDUKUNG KEPUTUSAN PENILAIAN KINERJA KARYAWAN BERBASIS AHP-SAW TERINTEGRASI SOP KEDISIPLINAN

Adli Zain Pribadi (Lead Researcher / Author) • Advised by: Yudi Herdiana, Yusuf Muharam
Program Studi Teknik Informatika, Universitas Bale Bandung • Industrial Case Study: Reegiolla The Shoes (Cimaung, Kab. Bandung)
Abstract Summary: Employee performance appraisal is a crucial aspect in human resource governance to sustain operational productivity in manufacturing industries. Conventional spreadsheet-based appraisal often introduces risks of attendance manipulation, subjective bias, and administrative delays. This research integrates the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to determine mathematically consistent weighting of criteria alongside Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) for multi-attribute ranking, successfully eliminating bias and producing fully auditable appraisal decisions aligned with company disciplinary Standard Operating Procedures (SOP).
Focus Areas: Decision Support Systems (DSS) AHP Weighting SAW Normalization HR Analytics Industrial Engineering
